HOW TO PLAY

// TOUCH

  • TAP boardRotate clockwise
  • SWIPE ← / →Move 1 column. Longer swipe = more columns
  • SWIPE ↓ & holdSoft drop
  • FLICK ↓ fastHard drop (instant)
  • TAP HOLD boxStore piece / swap. Once per drop
  • II buttonPause

// KEYBOARD

  • ← →Move
  • ↑ / XRotate (Z = counter-clockwise)
  • ↓Soft drop
  • SPACEHard drop
  • C / SHIFTHold
  • P / ESCPause

// SCORING

1 line = 100 · 2 = 300 · 3 = 500 · 4 = 800, × level. A new level every 10 lines, and the blocks fall faster.
